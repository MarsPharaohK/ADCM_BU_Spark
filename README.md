# ADCM

ADCM is a guided web tool for students who design with generative AI. It helps students make decisions about AI outputs, trace those outputs back to the assumptions that produced them, and change the conditions of generation on purpose. It also gives instructors a readable record of each student's process.

ADCM was designed and developed by Lingyi Kong.

## Background

The tool implements two frameworks developed by Lingyi Kong.

- **Backward by Design.** A pedagogical framework that pairs backward design with systems thinking. Students treat an AI output as evidence of the prompts, assumptions, and conditions that produced it. The framework was first published as "Backward by Design: Reframing AI Literacy through Systems Thinking and Critique Pedagogy" on Design Incubation on February 1, 2026, and presented at the Design Incubation Colloquium at the CAA Annual Conference on February 19, 2026.
- **AI-Assisted Design Competency Matrix (ADCM).** An assessment model that evaluates the reasoning process behind AI-assisted design work rather than the polish of the final artifact. It has four dimensions: exploratory breadth, reflective depth, systemic coherence, and executional refinement.

## How it works

Students move through five steps. Steps 2 to 4 form a loop that repeats as many times as needed.

1. **Finish line.** Define what success looks like on demo day before prompting anything. Choose three keywords that guide every later decision.
2. **Generate.** Produce three to five directions with any AI tool, then compare them against the keywords.
3. **Trace it back.** For a chosen output, record whether it fits the keywords, what it assumed, and why that assumption might seem normal. Each answer is treated as a hypothesis to test.
4. **Change the rules.** Either change the design, or change the conditions that produced it: the prompt, a constraint, the intended audience, or the finish line.
5. **Show.** Compare the first and latest versions, record optional user testing, and export the process as an editable slide deck.

Instructors can review each student's timeline and attach feedback to a specific record. Each record maps to the ADCM dimensions.

## Status

Pilot version, first released in October 2026 for use in design hackathons.

## Data and consent

Participants agree to a data protocol before they start. Records are used for instructor feedback and for research on how students design with AI.
